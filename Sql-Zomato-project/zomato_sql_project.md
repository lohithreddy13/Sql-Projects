# 🍽️ Zomato SQL Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior, sales performance, product trends, and loyalty program effectiveness using SQL on a simulated Zomato transactional database.

The objective of this project was to solve real-world business problems that product-based companies commonly face, such as:
- understanding customer purchasing behavior
- identifying high-performing products
- evaluating loyalty program effectiveness
- measuring customer engagement
- analyzing revenue contribution
- improving customer retention strategies

Using advanced SQL queries, raw transactional data was transformed into actionable business insights.

---

# 🎯 Business Problems Solved

## 1. Customer Spending Analysis
- Calculated total amount spent by each customer
- Identified high-value customers contributing most revenue
- Measured customer revenue contribution

## 2. Customer Engagement Analysis
- Measured customer purchase frequency
- Calculated active purchasing days per customer
- Analyzed customer activity patterns

## 3. Product Performance Analysis
- Identified most purchased products
- Found favorite products for each customer
- Analyzed product demand trends
- Measured product popularity across customers

## 4. Customer Purchase Journey Analysis
- Tracked first product purchased by each customer
- Analyzed customer buying sequence
- Used ranking functions to understand transaction order

## 5. Gold Membership Program Analysis
- Analyzed customer purchases before and after becoming gold members
- Identified first product purchased after membership activation
- Identified last product purchased before joining membership
- Measured customer loyalty behavior

## 6. Revenue & Order Insights
- Calculated total orders generated by customers
- Measured revenue generated from purchases
- Evaluated customer contribution toward business growth

## 7. Reward Points & Cashback Analytics
- Implemented custom reward point calculation logic
- Calculated cashback earned by customers
- Identified products generating maximum reward points
- Evaluated reward system effectiveness

## 8. Time-Based Loyalty Analytics
- Analyzed customer spending during the first year of membership
- Calculated loyalty points earned during membership periods
- Compared member purchasing behavior over time

## 9. Transaction Ranking & Customer Segmentation
- Ranked customer transactions using window functions
- Segmented gold-member and non-member transactions
- Analyzed behavioral differences between customer groups

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations can help improve customer retention and revenue growth:

### ✅ Improve Loyalty Program Targeting
Customers showed different purchasing behaviors before and after becoming gold members. Personalized loyalty rewards can increase retention and engagement.

### ✅ Promote High-Performing Products
The most frequently purchased products can be strategically promoted through bundles, recommendations, and targeted campaigns.

### ✅ Focus on High-Value Customers
Customers contributing the highest revenue should receive personalized offers, premium memberships, and retention-focused campaigns.

### ✅ Optimize Reward Point System
Products generating higher reward points can be leveraged to encourage repeat purchases and improve customer lifetime value.

### ✅ Increase Customer Engagement
Analyzing purchase frequency helps identify inactive customers who can be targeted with re-engagement campaigns.
